Foundations

Colours with measured contrast, type, spacing, radius, shadows and motion: the tokens Sassy is built from.

Colours

Warm stone neutrals do almost all the work. Moss is the one accent, and five muted accent families exist for app colours. Every pair below is measured with WCAG 2 contrast from the tokens when the site is built.

Text on surfaces

Body, secondary and meta text against the background and the three surfaces. The requirement is 4.5:1 for text; --fg-3 on --surface-3 carries only non-text marks (3:1).

Shadows

Low, diffuse and warm. Borders carry structure; a shadow only means “lifted”. Dark mode uses deeper shadows plus a hairline light ring.

Motion

Calm and short: 120 ms for hover and press, 200 ms for toggles and tabs, 320 ms for dialogs and toasts. Nothing bounces. Under prefers-reduced-motion every duration is 0.
