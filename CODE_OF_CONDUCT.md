# Code of Conduct

Sassy follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, give and take feedback on the work and not the person, and make room for people who are new. Harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, and publishing other people's private information are not accepted.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through GitHub: use *Report content* on the comment or issue, or the private vulnerability/contact option on the repository. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, close issues or block people who break this code, in line with the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.

---

**Norsk:** Vær vennlig og tålmodig, gi tilbakemelding på arbeidet og ikke personen, og gi plass til nye. Trakassering og deling av andres private opplysninger godtas ikke. Meld fra privat via GitHub.
